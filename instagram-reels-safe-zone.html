Why the 4:5 Feed Crop Destroys Reels Views

When viewers scroll through the Instagram main feed or their profile grid, Reels do not play in full 9:16 vertical ratio. Instead, Instagram crops the top 285px and bottom 285px to render a 4:5 frame ($1080 \times 1350$). If your initial 3-second hook or bold title is placed near the top or bottom of your 9:16 canvas, it gets chopped off completely in feed thumbnails, causing click-through rates to plummet.
