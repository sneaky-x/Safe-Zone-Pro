The Anatomy of a TikTok 9:16 Video Frame

TikTok is the most UI-dense vertical video platform. Native icons cover approximately 38% of the available viewing area on standard smartphone screens (such as iPhone 15/16 and Samsung Galaxy S24). If your video relies on on-screen text, titles, subtitles, or stickers placed incorrectly, viewers cannot read them, causing immediate swipe-aways.

Top Header (0 to 210px)

Contains the LIVE stream button, Friends / Following / For You tabs, and search magnifying glass. Never place logos or hook text in the top 210 pixels.

Right Action Column (X: 910 to 1080px)

Houses creator avatar with follow plus badge, like heart, comment bubble, bookmark ribbon, share arrow, and the spinning music disc. Leave a 170px right margin.

TikTok Safe Area Best Practices

  • Hook Safe Zone: Place your primary video hook between Y: 400px and Y: 900px, centered horizontally.
  • Subtitle Placement: Keep captions between Y: 1100px and Y: 1450px. This prevents overlap with the creator's username and sound marquee.
  • DaVinci Resolve & Premiere Tip: Create a transparent PNG guide of the safe zone and drop it on Track V2 during video editing. Toggle visibility off before final rendering!
