Every major vertical video platform operates at standard 1080 × 1920 pixels (9:16 aspect ratio). Here are the exact pixel obstruction boundaries to protect your subtitles, logos, and viral hooks.

CapCut & Mobile Video Editor Best Practices

Mobile creators using CapCut or InShot often place auto-captions too low. Follow these golden rules:

  • Subtitle Elevation: Keep auto-captions at least 25% up from the bottom of the canvas (above Y: 1440px).
  • Center Your 3-Second Hook: Position your visual punchline inside the 800×1200 center safe box.
  • Right Margin Guard: Never let text extend beyond 900px wide on the right side to prevent like button blockage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything video editors need to know about vertical safe zone compliance.

What is the 9:16 vertical video safe zone?
The 9:16 safe zone is the unobstructed display area inside a 1080×1920 vertical video frame. Social platforms overlay native buttons (like, comment, share, bookmark, sound disc), creator handles, and bottom navigation bars that obstruct approximately 35% of the screen. Placing titles and subtitles inside the safe zone guarantees that every viewer can read your content across all mobile devices.
Why do Instagram Reels get cropped to 4:5 in the Explore feed?
While Instagram Reels playback is full 9:16 (1080×1920), when a Reel appears in the home feed or profile explore grid, Instagram displays it in a 4:5 ratio (1080×1350). The top 285 pixels and bottom 285 pixels are clipped. Social Safe-Zone Pro includes an amber 4:5 crop guide so you can ensure your thumbnail and visual hooks remain perfectly centered.
